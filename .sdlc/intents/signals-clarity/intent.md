# Intent: `Signals, one step at a time`

**Status:** in progress (2026-11-08) · **Owner:** `@ofri-peretz` · **Builds on:** [relay-engagement](../relay-engagement/intent.md)

## What is wanted (the presenter, 2026-11-08)

Someone in the room may not know what stdout, a trace or a severity is. Nothing is left to the
presenter's voice alone: every concept, from the most fundamental to the most advanced, is shown on
the big screen, the phones or both, one step at a time, with a clear example, live where possible.
Visual, calm, never looping, never a "WTF" moment. Junior to senior: plain first, then deep enough
that a senior learns something (costs, sync vs async, standards).

## Done when

- Every term on the Signals path is taught (a step or a hands-on scene) before any title, lead,
  phone line or "how" uses it. `relay/test.mjs` enforces the order.
- Each explainer is one idea: a title, one lead, one picture, optionally the participant's own data.
- Technical claims hold up to a senior: real span names, correct code, correct prices with dates,
  histograms described as estimates.
- The path fits 60 minutes at a real speaking pace.
